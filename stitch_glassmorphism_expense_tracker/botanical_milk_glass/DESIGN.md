## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, serene financial sanctuary designed to dismantle the stress, clinical austerity, and hyperactive urgency typical of modern fintech. Rooted in an organic, tactile aesthetic inspired by minimalist art monographs, botanical ateliers, and high-craft apothecary packaging, it treats personal wealth management as a mindful, grounded ritual.

The visual dialect pairs translucent milk-glass panels with raw linen textures, warm ceramic undertones, and ink-inspired editorial typography. Rather than alarming red badges and glaring fluorescent green tickers, information is delivered with restraint, quiet authority, and spatial elegance. Visual layers feel physical—diffused glass resting on raw woven cloth, catching soft natural perimeter light.

The design movement combines **Glassmorphism** and **Tactile Minimalism**:
- Silky, semi-opaque milk-glass surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`) floating above oatmeal and soft linen ground planes.
- Micro-fine hairline boundaries mimicking hand-trimmed deckle paper or ground-glass bevels.
- Rich, earthy botanical accents that convey natural growth, resilience, and balance.

## Colors

The palette is tuned entirely for organic light-mode harmony. Pure digital black (`#000000`) and sterile white (`#FFFFFF`) are banished from large-scale fills, replaced with naturally pigmented ink tones, chalks, and unbleached fiber hues.

### Foundational Palette
- **Canvas Base (`#F6F4EE`)**: Warm unbleached oatmeal, serving as the universal application background.
- **Canvas Muted / Recessed (`#EFECE4`)**: Soft wet-linen tone for sunken containers, toolbars, and segmented control wells.
- **Milk Glass Fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`)**: High-luminance frosted surface allowing canvas warmth to softly register underneath.
- **Glass Rim Highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`)**: Subtly luminous top-and-left border reflecting ambient daylight.
- **Glass Rim Shadow (`rgba(43, 38, 33, 0.06)`)**: Whispering earthen border on bottom-and-right edges for physical seating.

### Inks & Typography
- **Pine Ink (`#18251D`)**: Deep forest tone reserved for large financial totals, display headings, and primary visual anchors.
- **Espresso Ink (`#2B2621`)**: Warm, rich charcoal-brown for section titles, active labels, and critical values.
- **Muted Sage (`#5A665D`)**: Grounded herbal grey for secondary descriptions, timestamps, table column headers, and inactive states.
- **Faint Linen Line (`#E4DFD3`)**: Muted structural stroke for dividers and non-glass boundaries.

### Accents & Financial Signifiers
- **Botanical Olive (`#4D6B3C`)**: Primary brand interactive color; authoritative, rooted, and organic.
- **Moss Accent (`#5C7F48`)**: Interactive hover and focus state for actionable components.
- **Pistachio Wash (`#D8E5CF`)**: Tinted container backdrop for active chips, category pills, and selected states.
- **Eucalyptus Cashflow (`#3B7A57`)**: Serene, dignified green indicating positive yields, dividend inflows, and savings deposits.
- **Terracotta Alert (`#C9704F`)**: Gentle earthen red-ochre for budget warnings, payment due dates, and downward variances. Replaces harsh signal-reds.

## Typography

The type hierarchy uses **Inter** with deliberate Swiss precision, relying on generous tracking in small metadata and tightening negative tracking in massive financial balances. Weight distribution is purposefully restrained: regular (400) and medium (500) do the foundational work, avoiding heavy weights (700+) to keep the composition airy, calm, and uncluttered.

- **Financial Figures**: Main net worth and account overview numbers apply `headline-xl` using tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve optical stillness when figures fluctuate.
- **Section Headers & Ledger Groupings**: Rendered in `label-caps` in uppercase, colored with `Muted Sage`, creating a clean archival ledger feel.
- **Body & Metadata**: `body-md` handles standard transaction descriptions in `Espresso Ink`, while contextual tags, category notes, and dates use `body-sm` in `Muted Sage`.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an airy, asymmetric editorial structure built upon an 8px base rhythm. Negative space is treated as an active luxury material rather than void space to be filled.

### Spatial Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column responsive fluid grid with max-width constrained to `1360px`, `gutter` of `1.5rem` (24px), and page canvas `margin` of `3rem` (48px). Cards and analytical tiles group naturally into 4, 6, or 8-column spreads.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column fluid grid, `gutter` of `1rem` (16px), and outer canvas `margin` of `2rem` (32px).
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with outer `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem` (20px) and `gutter-sm` of `1rem` (16px). All stacked cards stretch across the full column span.

### Padding Principles
Cards leverage spacious internal breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to prevent data congestion. Dense tables are avoided in favor of conversational, grouped transaction lists separated by soft linen dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through translucent physical layering and diffused light rather than harsh drop shadows. Elements appear as softly polished milk-glass slabs floating millimetres over an unbleached linen ground.

### Layer 0: The Base Canvas
The canvas is tinted oatmeal (`#F6F4EE`), occasionally accented by ultra-subtle, expansive radial gradients of warm cream (`#FAF8F3`) behind key analytical zones to create daylight variation across the screen.

### Layer 1: Milk Glass Cards (Resting)
- **Background**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`
- **Backdrop Blur**: `blur(18px) saturate(140%)`
- **Border**: Compound 1px hairline border:
  - Top & Left: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` (morning sunlight rim)
  - Bottom & Right: `1px solid rgba(228, 223, 211, 0.65)` (linen ground contact)
- **Ambient Shadow**: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(43, 38, 33, 0.04), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(43, 38, 33, 0.02)`

### Layer 2: Elevated & Interactive Glass (Hover, Modals, Drawers)
- **Background**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`
- **Backdrop Blur**: `blur(24px) saturate(150%)`
- **Border**: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`
- **Ambient Shadow**: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(43, 38, 33, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(43, 38, 33, 0.03)`
- **Transition**: Smooth organic curve `transform 280ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), box-shadow 280ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.

### Layer Sunken: Embedded Well Controls
Segmented filters, search bars, and toggle backgrounds sit slightly recessed into the linen:
- **Background**: `#EFECE4`
- **Border**: `1px solid rgba(228, 223, 211, 0.8)`
- **Shadow**: `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(43, 38, 33, 0.03)`

## Shapes

The geometry uses generous rounded contours balanced by crisp internal structural elements:

- **Cards & Primary Containers**: Scaled to `rounded-2xl` (`1.25rem` to `1.5rem` / 20px - 24px) to mimic softly tumbled sea glass or hand-formed ceramic coasters.
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags**: Utilize smooth organic radiuses (`0.75rem` / 12px) for medium elements, while status chips, search pills, and inline metrics use full continuous pill radius (`9999px`).
- **Input Fields**: Softly grounded with `0.75rem` (12px) curvature to retain a structured, intentional form.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Rich Botanical Olive moss (`#4D6B3C`) fill with pure porcelain text (`#FAF8F3`). Hover transitions to `#5C7F48` with subtle lift (`translateY(-1px)`). Focus ring uses a double boundary: a 2px oatmeal gap and a 2px Olive moss outline.
- **Secondary Glass Action**: Semi-translucent milk glass fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`), crisp white edge border, and Espresso ink text (`#2B2621`). On hover, opacity shifts to `0.92`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent fill with `Espresso Ink` text, adopting a soft `#EFECE4` linen wash on hover.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed from `rounded-2xl` milk-glass panels with `space-lg` to `space-xl` inner padding.
- Card titles sit in `headline-sm` (`#18251D`), while associated helper metrics and status metadata flank the right header edge in `label-caps` (`#5A665D`).
- Inner card dividers are rendered with `1px solid rgba(228, 223, 211, 0.5)` with `space-md` vertical clearance.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Inactive**: Sunken linen tone (`#EFECE4`), muted sage text (`#5A665D`), borderless.
- **Active / Selected**: Warm pistachio tint (`#D8E5CF`), deep botanical olive text (`#18251D`), reinforced by a micro border in `rgba(77, 107, 60, 0.2)`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Container**: Translucent milk-tinted background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), 1px linen border (`#E4DFD3`), `rounded-lg` (12px).
- **Text & Placeholder**: Input value in `Pine Ink` (`#18251D`), placeholder in soft `Muted Sage` (`#8C9990`).
- **Focus State**: Background shifts to pure milky white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`), border transitions to Botanical Olive (`#4D6B3C`), accompanied by an ambient olive glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(77, 107, 60, 0.12)`.

### Lists & Ledger Rows
- Minimalist ledger rows free of cluttered vertical dividers.
- Rows use `1px` bottom borders in `rgba(228, 223, 211, 0.6)`.
- Hovering over a transaction row gently reveals an ultra-thin highlight wash (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`) across the item bounding box.
- Left slot: Merchant/institution avatar with a circular linen background (`#EFECE4`) and minimalist botanical icon.
- Right slot: Numeric cash flow. Inflows appear in serene Eucalyptus (`#3B7A57`, prefixed with `+`), while normal expenses remain in standard Pine Ink (`#18251D`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular or soft-corner boxes with an unselected `#E4DFD3` border on linen background.
- Selected state fills with Botanical Olive moss (`#4D6B3C`) displaying a chalk-white checkmark or center pip.

### Financial Metric Callout (Specialized Component)
- Distinct display cards featuring a prominent numeric total in `headline-xl`, a small descriptive title in `label-caps`, and an organic delta capsule.
- Positive deltas are housed in a faint eucalyptus wash (`rgba(59, 122, 87, 0.1)`) with `#3B7A57` typography.
- Terracotta alerts sit inside an ochre wash (`rgba(201, 112, 79, 0.1)`) with `#C9704F` text for gentle advisory notice.
