## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines personal wealth tracking through an organic, grounding lens. Moving decisively away from cold, hyper-analytical neon dashboards and sterile neo-banking aesthetics, it establishes an environment of sanctuary, patience, and mindful prosperity.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Glassmorphism** with **Quiet Luxury Minimalism**:
- Translucent dark spruce surfaces float above deep woodland tones, creating soft visual depth without harsh borders.
- The visual voice is calming, restorative, and human-centric, designed to reduce financial anxiety through low-stimulation contrast, tactile soft glows, and organic botanical hues.
- Interfaces feel akin to an architectural greenhouse at dusk: deep earthy foundations, delicate diffused glass planes, and warm woven cashmere accents.

## Colors

The palette relies on organic, deeply rooted hues that evoke natural woodland strata paired with tactile textiles:

### Base Surfaces & Canvas
- **Canvas Base:** `#111613` (Deep Woodland Charcoal) — the absolute floor of the UI.
- **Surface Mid:** `#151C17` — intermediate container layers and deep inset regions.
- **Surface High / Elevated:** `#1A231D` — high-level backgrounds and secondary backing cards.
- **Frosted Glass Cards:** `rgba(28, 38, 32, 0.65)` layered with subtle highlight borders `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

### Accent & Textural Hues
- **Primary / Moss:** `#8FA978` (Olive Moss) to `#97B082` (Warm Pistachio) — interactive controls, key balance accents, and primary emphasis.
- **Primary Glow:** `rgba(126, 154, 104, 0.25)` — soft atmospheric aura for active states.
- **Cashmere Cream (Primary Text/Highlight):** `#F5EFEB` — warm off-white for headers, hero values, and primary metrics.
- **Parchment Neutral (Secondary Text):** `#C7C0B8` — calm, low-glare neutral for body copy and supporting labels.
- **Subdued Muted Neutral:** `#7E887E` — tertiary metadata and inactive controls.

### Functional & Semantic Hues
- **Positive / Inflow:** `#52B788` (Crisp Leaf Green) — denotes wealth accumulation, successful deposits, and positive growth.
- **Attention / Pending:** `#E9B44C` (Warm Dijon / Honey Amber) — indicates pending clearances, review triggers, and neutral alerts.
- **Negative / Outflow / Destructive:** `#C85A48` (Muted Terracotta Clay) — subdued, natural earthen red for expenses, debit balances, and destructive actions.

## Typography

Typography is set exclusively in **Inter**, styled to express an editorial, tactile poise rather than mechanical data presentation:

- **Tabular Figures:** Always apply `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` across all data rows, financial ledger tables, currency counters, and timestamps to prevent visual jitter when amounts change.
- **Gentle Tracked Labels:** Micro-labels and category trackers utilize uppercase or capitalized formats with gentle tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) in weights `500` and `600`, rendering them legible without overpowering metric numerals.
- **Warm Cashmere Hierarchy:** Primary headings and monetary balances use `#F5EFEB` with generous line-heights. Secondary and descriptive metadata uses `#C7C0B8`. Avoid harsh absolute white (`#FFFFFF`) in body prose to preserve the low-glare organic atmosphere.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an airy, rhythmic grid designed to simulate physical breathing room between complex financial figures:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `2.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Maximum content width caps at `1280px` centered to maintain intimate, distraction-free scanning.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Multi-column metric cards stack into 2-up configurations.
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column layout utilizing tight `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Dense metric panels collapse into horizontal swipe reels or vertical card stacks with minimum `48px` tap targets.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Built on an intentional 4px base increment. Padding inside frosted containers remains generous (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) to emphasize the feeling of unhurried luxury.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent material tiers and soft botanical luminescence rather than standard opaque dropshadows:

### 1. Canvas Tier
The foundational substrate (`#111613`) represents the physical surface beneath all components. Subtly tinted gradients from `#151C17` can be anchored in page corners to simulate soft overhead illumination through foliage.

### 2. Frosted Spruce Glass (Primary Plane)
- **Background:** `rgba(28, 38, 32, 0.65)`
- **Backdrop Filter:** `blur(12px) saturate(120%)`
- **Border:** `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (simulating light caught on beveled glass edges)
- **Shadow:** `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`

### 3. Elevated Modals & Floating Shelves
- **Background:** `rgba(32, 44, 37, 0.85)`
- **Backdrop Filter:** `blur(20px)`
- **Border:** `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
- **Shadow:** `0 16px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(126, 154, 104, 0.12)` (subtle moss-tinted back-bloom)

### 4. Interactive State Illumination
Hover and active states never produce sudden harsh fills; instead, they deepen card luminosity (`rgba(35, 48, 40, 0.75)`) and brighten the border outline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`), with buttons exuding a soft localized glow (`0 0 20px rgba(126, 154, 104, 0.3)`).

## Shapes

The design language uses organic, hand-softened geometry:
- Standard cards, dashboard tiles, and modal windows adopt `rounded-2xl` (16px), giving glass sheets a polished stone or sea-glass tactile quality.
- Internal control elements such as inputs, secondary buttons, and segment tabs adopt `rounded-lg` (8px).
- Status tags, indicators, and tertiary chips use full organic pills (`rounded-full`) to clearly signal micro-actions and semantic states.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Warm pistachio-moss fill (`#8FA978`), text in deep charcoal (`#111613`), weight `600`. On hover, transitions to `#97B082` accompanied by a soft botanical glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(126, 154, 104, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary (Glass):** Frosted dark spruce background (`rgba(28, 38, 32, 0.65)`), border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, text `#F5EFEB`. Hover triggers background brightness shift to `rgba(40, 54, 46, 0.8)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No fill or border; text in `#C7C0B8`, transitioning to `#F5EFEB` with an underlined warm pistachio rule on focus.

### Cards & Ledger Modules
- Built using the primary glass recipe (`16px` border-radius, `12px` backdrop blur, `rgba(28, 38, 32, 0.65)` fill, `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Interior padding adheres to `space-lg` (24px) on desktop and `space-md` (16px) on mobile devices.
- Subtle inner gradient highlights (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.04) 0%, transparent 100%)`) can line the top edge for a tactile glass bevel.

### Inputs & Select Fields
- Containers use deep inset styling (`#111613` at 70% opacity) inset inside glass cards, with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Text color is `#F5EFEB`, placeholder is `#7E887E`.
- Focus state eliminates standard blue rings; instead, uses `border-color: #8FA978` with a diffused glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(143, 169, 120, 0.2)`.
- Financial amount inputs automatically render in tabular numeral format with currency symbols locked in `#C7C0B8`.

### Chips & Semantic Status Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.25rem 0.75rem`, font size `11px`, weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`.
- **Income / Settled:** Background `rgba(82, 183, 136, 0.12)`, text `#52B788`, border `1px solid rgba(82, 183, 136, 0.25)`.
- **Pending / In Review:** Background `rgba(233, 180, 76, 0.12)`, text `#E9B44C`, border `1px solid rgba(233, 180, 76, 0.25)`.
- **Expense / Outflow:** Background `rgba(200, 90, 72, 0.12)`, text `#C85A48`, border `1px solid rgba(200, 90, 72, 0.25)`.

### Lists & Ledger Rows
- Table rows and transaction items use full-bleed horizontal dividing borders of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- Row hover states reveal an understated gradient pill background of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`.
- Transaction values display aligned right with explicit mathematical prefixing (`+` in `#52B788`, `−` in `#F5EFEB` or `#C85A48`).

### Checkboxes & Toggle Controls
- Box dimensions are `18px × 18px` with `4px` rounded corners.
- Unchecked: `background: rgba(17, 22, 19, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`.
- Checked: `background: #8FA978`, checkmark icon `#111613`, box-shadow `0 0 8px rgba(126, 154, 104, 0.3)`.

### Allocation & Cashflow Progress Rings
- Track bars feature deep spruce troughs (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Progress fills utilize an organic horizontal gradient from Olive Moss (`#7E9A68`) to Warm Pistachio (`#97B082`) with rounded end-caps.
